        In-game settings

        Everything is adjustable in game from the FLICK menu (F7), under Aetherial Bloodlines. Racial drawbacks can be switched off entirely; the racial abilities are part of the bargain those drawbacks pay for, so they switch off with them. Drawback mastery can be switched off for permanent drawbacks. The Bosmer's Green Pact can block harvesting, allow it with a penalty, or be turned off. The level-up gains for every race can be changed on their own page, and modded races can be added in the INI.
